Reference 0014: Describing People and Things

Quick-reference sheet for Lesson 0014. Print-friendly.

Describe and locate

  • Quien es ella? = Who is she?
  • Es una vecina muy amable. = She is a very kind neighbor.
  • Donde esta la iglesia? = Where is the church?
  • Esta cerca de aqui. = It is near here.

Useful descriptions

  • amable = kind
  • grande / pequeno = big / small
  • cerca / lejos = near / far
  • nuevo / viejo = new / old

Pronunciation reminders

  • quien is kyehn.
  • vecina has four clear syllables: veh-SEE-na.
  • Keep esta cerca together as one location phrase.
