Reference 0001: Core Greetings and Mission Openers

Quick-reference sheet for Lesson 0001. Print-friendly.

Greeting block

  • Buenos dias = Good morning
  • Buenas tardes = Good afternoon
  • Buenas noches = Good evening/night
  • Mucho gusto = Nice to meet you

How are you + basic responses

  • Como esta? = How are you? (formal)
  • Estoy bien = I am well
  • Estoy muy bien = I am very well
  • Mas o menos = So-so

Mission-context openers

  • Somos misioneros de La Iglesia de Jesucristo de los Santos de los Ultimos Dias.
  • Podemos compartir un mensaje de Jesucristo?
  • Como se llama usted?

Pronunciation reminders

  • j sounds like a strong English h in most dialects.
  • ll often sounds like y in many regions.
  • Stress usually falls on the second-last syllable unless marked.
