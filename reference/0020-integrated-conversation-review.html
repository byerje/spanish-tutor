Reference 0020: Integrated Conversation Review

Quick-reference sheet for Lesson 0020. Print-friendly.

Connect the conversation

  • Como esta su familia? = How is your family?
  • Ayer hablamos y fue un buen dia. = Yesterday we talked and it was a good day.
  • Manana podemos vernos a las cuatro? = Can we see each other tomorrow at four?
  • Si, y podemos compartir un mensaje. = Yes, and we can share a message.

Conversation connectors

  • y = and
  • ayer = yesterday
  • manana = tomorrow
  • podemos = we can

Pronunciation reminders

  • familia has four light syllables: fa-MEE-lya.
  • vernos is VEHR-nos.
  • Pause naturally between each idea, not every word.
