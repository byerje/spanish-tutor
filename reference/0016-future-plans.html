Reference 0016: Future Plans

Quick-reference sheet for Lesson 0016. Print-friendly.

Make plans

  • Manana vamos a visitar a Ana. = Tomorrow we are going to visit Ana.
  • Quieres venir con nosotros? = Do you want to come with us?
  • Si, puedo ir a las cuatro. = Yes, I can go at four.
  • Perfecto, nos vemos manana. = Perfect, see you tomorrow.

Plan words

  • manana = tomorrow
  • vamos a = we are going to
  • venir = to come
  • puedo = I can

Pronunciation reminders

  • visitar has stress on tar: vee-see-TAR.
  • venir is veh-NEER.
  • Link nos vemos manana as one friendly closing.
