Reference 0019: Simple Gospel Vocabulary

Quick-reference sheet for Lesson 0019. Print-friendly.

Core statements

  • Dios nos ama. = God loves us.
  • Jesucristo nos ayuda. = Jesus Christ helps us.
  • La fe trae esperanza. = Faith brings hope.
  • Te gustaria aprender mas? = Would you like to learn more?

Gospel words

  • Dios = God
  • Jesucristo = Jesus Christ
  • la fe = faith
  • la esperanza = hope

Pronunciation reminders

  • Dios is one clear syllable: DYOS.
  • Jesucristo can be spoken slowly in four parts.
  • Pause before the invitation to learn more.
