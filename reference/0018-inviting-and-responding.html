Reference 0018: Inviting and Responding

Quick-reference sheet for Lesson 0018. Print-friendly.

Invite and respond

  • Te gustaria tomar un cafe? = Would you like to have coffee?
  • Si, me gustaria mucho. = Yes, I would like that very much.
  • Lo siento, hoy no puedo. = I am sorry, I cannot today.
  • Tal vez manana por la tarde? = Maybe tomorrow afternoon?

Invitation words

  • tomar = to have / drink
  • caminar = to walk
  • hablar = to talk
  • tal vez = maybe

Pronunciation reminders

  • gustaria has four beats: goo-sta-REE-a.
  • mucho is MOO-cho.
  • Use lo siento gently, without sounding abrupt.
