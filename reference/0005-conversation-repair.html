Reference 0005: Conversation Repair

Quick-reference sheet for Lesson 0005. Print-friendly.

When you do not understand

  • No entiendo. = I do not understand.
  • Puede repetir, por favor? = Can you repeat, please?
  • Puede hablar mas despacio? = Can you speak more slowly?

When the message is clear

  • Ahora entiendo. = Now I understand.
  • Si, entiendo. = Yes, I understand.
  • Gracias. = Thank you.

Useful pattern

  • Puede + verb...? = Can you + verb...?
  • Puede repetir? = Can you repeat?
  • Puede hablar...? = Can you speak...?

Pronunciation reminders

  • entiendo sounds like en-tee-en-doh.
  • repetir has stress on the final syllable: re-pe-TEER.
  • Keep por favor together as a polite phrase.
