Reference 0015: Past Events

Quick-reference sheet for Lesson 0015. Print-friendly.

Tell what happened

  • Ayer fui al mercado. = Yesterday I went to the market.
  • Hable con una familia. = I spoke with a family.
  • Compartimos un mensaje sencillo. = We shared a simple message.
  • Fue un buen dia. = It was a good day.

Past-event words

  • ayer = yesterday
  • fui = I went
  • hable = I spoke
  • compartimos = we shared

Pronunciation reminders

  • ayer sounds like ah-YEHR.
  • fui is one quick syllable: fwee.
  • Keep buen dia bright and natural.
