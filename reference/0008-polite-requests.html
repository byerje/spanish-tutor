Reference 0008: Polite Requests

Quick-reference sheet for Lesson 0008. Print-friendly.

Useful requests

  • Me puede dar agua, por favor? = Can you give me water, please?
  • Me puede ayudar? = Can you help me?
  • Podemos pasar? = Can we come in?

Courtesy

  • por favor = please
  • Muchas gracias = Thank you very much
  • Con permiso = Excuse me / with your permission

Pattern to reuse

  • Me puede + verb...? = Can you + verb...?
  • Me puede decir...? = Can you tell me...?

Pronunciation reminders

  • puede sounds like PWEH-deh.
  • ayudar has stress on the final syllable: ayu-DAR.
  • Use a warm voice and a clear por favor.
