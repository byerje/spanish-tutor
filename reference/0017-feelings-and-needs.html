Reference 0017: Feelings and Needs

Quick-reference sheet for Lesson 0017. Print-friendly.

Care for someone

  • Como te sientes hoy? = How do you feel today?
  • Estoy un poco cansado. = I am a little tired.
  • Necesitas ayuda? = Do you need help?
  • Si, gracias. Eso es muy amable. = Yes, thank you. That is very kind.

Feeling words

  • bien = well
  • cansado = tired
  • preocupado = worried
  • enfermo = sick

Pronunciation reminders

  • sientes sounds like SYEHN-tehs.
  • ayuda is ah-YOO-da.
  • Use a gentle voice for Como te sientes?
