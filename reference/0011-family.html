Reference 0011: Family

Quick-reference sheet for Lesson 0011. Print-friendly.

Ask and answer

  • Tiene hermanos? = Do you have siblings?
  • Si, tengo un hermano. = Yes, I have one brother.
  • Como se llama? = What is his or her name?
  • Se llama Luis. = His or her name is Luis.

Family words

  • la familia = family
  • el hermano / la hermana = brother / sister
  • la madre / el padre = mother / father
  • el hijo / la hija = son / daughter

Pronunciation reminders

  • hermanos has a soft h: er-MA-nos.
  • ll in llama sounds like Y in many regions.
  • Keep Es muy amable warm and clear.
