Reference 0007: Places and Directions

Quick-reference sheet for Lesson 0007. Print-friendly.

Ask about a place

  • Donde esta la iglesia? = Where is the church?
  • Donde esta el bano? = Where is the bathroom?
  • Esta cerca de aqui. = It is near here.

Directions

  • Siga derecho = Go straight
  • Doble a la derecha = Turn right
  • A la izquierda = On the left
  • A la derecha = On the right

Pronunciation reminders

  • Donde sounds like DON-deh.
  • derecho has a soft ch sound like English church.
  • Keep a la derecha together as one phrase.
