Reference 0004: Likes and Preferences

Quick-reference sheet for Lesson 0004. Print-friendly.

Ask about interests

  • Que le gusta hacer? = What do you like to do?
  • Me gusta... = I like...
  • A mi me gusta... = I like... (emphasis)

Useful activities

  • escuchar musica = to listen to music
  • leer = to read
  • estudiar = to study
  • caminar = to walk
  • cocinar = to cook

Agree or disagree politely

  • A mi tambien. = Me too.
  • A mi no. = Not me.
  • Que interesante. = How interesting.

Pronunciation reminders

  • Que sounds like keh.
  • gusta has a soft g sound, like the g in go.
  • Keep me gusta together as one rhythm group.
