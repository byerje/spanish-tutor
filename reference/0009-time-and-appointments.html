Reference 0009: Time and Appointments

Quick-reference sheet for Lesson 0009. Print-friendly.

Make a plan

  • A que hora nos vemos? = What time will we see each other?
  • Manana esta bien? = Is tomorrow okay?
  • Nos vemos entonces. = See you then.

Useful times

  • a la una = at one
  • a las tres = at three
  • de la manana = in the morning
  • de la tarde = in the afternoon

Pronunciation reminders

  • hora sounds like OH-ra.
  • manana sounds like ma-NYA-na.
  • Link nos vemos together: nos-VEH-mos.
