Reference 0003: Talking About Today

Quick-reference sheet for Lesson 0003. Print-friendly.

Ask about today

  • Que hace usted hoy? = What are you doing today? (formal)
  • Y usted? = And you?

Share a plan

  • Hoy voy a trabajar. = Today I am going to work.
  • Hoy voy a visitar a una familia. = Today I am going to visit a family.
  • Hoy voy a estudiar. = Today I am going to study.
  • Hoy voy a descansar. = Today I am going to rest.

Pattern to reuse

  • Hoy voy a + activity = Today I am going to + activity
  • trabajar = to work
  • visitar = to visit
  • estudiar = to study
  • descansar = to rest

Pronunciation reminders

  • Que sounds like keh.
  • Hoy sounds like oy.
  • Link voy a together: voy-a.
