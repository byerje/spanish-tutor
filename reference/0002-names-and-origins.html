Reference 0002: Names and Origins

Quick-reference sheet for Lesson 0002. Print-friendly.

Ask and answer a name

  • Como se llama usted? = What is your name? (formal)
  • Me llamo [name]. = My name is [name].
  • Mucho gusto. = Nice to meet you.

Ask and answer an origin

  • De donde es usted? = Where are you from? (formal)
  • Soy de [place]. = I am from [place].
  • Somos de [place]. = We are from [place].

Useful places

  • Mexico = Mexico
  • los Estados Unidos = the United States
  • Texas = Texas
  • [your city] = [your city]

Pronunciation reminders

  • ll in llamo often sounds like y in many regions.
  • j in trabajo and Jesucristo sounds like a strong English h in most dialects.
  • Keep de short and connected: soy de Mexico.
