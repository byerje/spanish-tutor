Reference 0013: Weather

Quick-reference sheet for Lesson 0013. Print-friendly.

Talk about today

  • Que tiempo hace hoy? = What is the weather like today?
  • Hace calor y hay sol. = It is hot and sunny.
  • Vamos a llevar una chaqueta? = Shall we bring a jacket?
  • Si, buena idea. = Yes, good idea.

Weather words

  • hace frio = it is cold
  • hace calor = it is hot
  • hay sol = it is sunny
  • hay lluvia = there is rain

Pronunciation reminders

  • tiempo sounds like TYEHM-po.
  • ll in llevar is often like Y.
  • Keep buena idea linked and friendly.
