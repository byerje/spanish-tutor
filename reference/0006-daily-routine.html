Reference 0006: Daily Routine

Quick-reference sheet for Lesson 0006. Print-friendly.

Routine actions

  • Me despierto = I wake up
  • Desayuno = I eat breakfast
  • Estudio espanol = I study Spanish
  • Me acuesto = I go to bed

Times and parts of day

  • a las seis = at six
  • a las siete = at seven
  • por la manana = in the morning
  • a las diez = at ten

Pattern to reuse

  • Me despierto a las [time]. = I wake up at [time].
  • Estudio [subject] por la manana. = I study [subject] in the morning.

Pronunciation reminders

  • despierto sounds like des-pyehr-toh.
  • manana sounds like ma-NYA-na.
  • Keep me acuesto together as one rhythm group.
