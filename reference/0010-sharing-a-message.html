Reference 0010: Sharing a Message

Quick-reference sheet for Lesson 0010. Print-friendly.

Invite and introduce

  • Podemos compartir un mensaje breve? = Can we share a brief message?
  • Es un mensaje sobre Jesucristo. = It is a message about Jesus Christ.
  • Que piensa usted? = What do you think? (formal)

Follow up

  • Podemos regresar otro dia? = Can we come back another day?
  • Si, por favor. = Yes, please.
  • Gracias por su tiempo. = Thank you for your time.

Pronunciation reminders

  • breve has two clear syllables: BREH-veh.
  • piensa sounds like PYEHN-sa.
  • Keep sobre Jesucristo connected in one thought group.
