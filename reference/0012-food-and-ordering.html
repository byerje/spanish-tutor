Reference 0012: Food and Ordering

Quick-reference sheet for Lesson 0012. Print-friendly.

Order simply

  • Quiero una sopa, por favor. = I want a soup, please.
  • Tambien quiero agua. = I also want water.
  • Cuanto cuesta? = How much does it cost?
  • Gracias, esta muy bien. = Thank you, that is very good.

Food words

  • el pan = bread
  • la fruta = fruit
  • el cafe = coffee
  • la comida = food / meal

Pronunciation reminders

  • quiero sounds like kyeh-ro.
  • agua starts with AH-gua.
  • Say por favor as one smooth polite phrase.
