# Mission: Conversational Spanish Fluency for Mission Service

## Why
My wife and I plan to serve a mission for The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in about two years. We want to communicate effectively in spoken Spanish from the beginning, likely in Mexico, rather than waiting many months to understand and be understood.

## Success looks like
- Hold a 10-minute spoken conversation with a native speaker about daily routines.
- Hold a 10-minute spoken conversation about gospel-related topics with understandable pronunciation and responses.
- Understand and respond to common questions in real time during everyday interactions.

## Constraints
- Lessons should be short: 5-10 minutes each.
- Every lesson should teach one focused concept and include practice plus an end-of-lesson assessment.
- Reinforce weak areas in future lessons based on assessment performance.
- Use only no-cost resources.

## Out of scope
- Deep grammar theory in the first phase.
- Advanced or literary Spanish early on.
- Over-prioritizing passive vocabulary without speaking and listening practice.
