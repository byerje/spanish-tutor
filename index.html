Welcome!

Course goal: Build understandable spoken Spanish for everyday conversations and missionary service through short, focused practice lessons.

Choose any lesson to begin, review, or revisit. Each lesson builds on conversational scenarios you'll encounter in real-world situations.

Start with Lesson 0001 if you are new to the course. Use the assessment in each lesson to decide whether to continue or review.
