# Conversational Spanish for Mission Service Resources

## Knowledge

- [FSI Spanish Basic Course (Public Domain)](https://www.fsi-language-courses.org/fsi-spanish-basic-course/)
  Structured, drill-heavy training focused on speaking and listening. Use for: high-repetition oral pattern practice.

- [SpanishPod101 beginner Spanish](https://www.spanishpod101.com/)
  Beginner-focused audio lessons with transcripts and patterned speaking drills. Use for: short spoken practice and useful everyday phrases.

- [Diccionario de la lengua espanola (RAE)](https://dle.rae.es/)
  Authoritative Spanish dictionary from Real Academia Espanola. Use for: checking precise meaning and usage.

- [FundeuRAE recommendations](https://www.fundeu.es/recomendaciones/)
  Practical Spanish usage guidance. Use for: real-world wording and correctness questions.

- [Instituto Cervantes resources](https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/)
  High-trust Spanish teaching resources. Use for: grammar/usage references when needed.

- [Spanish pronunciation guide (Forvo)](https://forvo.com/languages/es/)
  Native-speaker pronunciation examples. Use for: pronunciation spot checks on specific words.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/Spanish](https://www.reddit.com/r/Spanish/)
  Large moderated Spanish-learning community. Use for: practical learning tips, common pitfalls, and accountability.

- [Language Exchange on Reddit (r/language_exchange)](https://www.reddit.com/r/language_exchange/)
  Peer exchanges for real conversation practice. Use for: speaking practice with native speakers.

- [HelloTalk community](https://www.hellotalk.com/)
  Large language exchange network (free tier available). Use for: short text/voice exchanges and correction.
