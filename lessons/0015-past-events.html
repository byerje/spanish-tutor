Lesson 0015: Past Events

Target time: 5-10 minutes. Mission link: share what happened during a visit or service day.

Tangible win for today: You can say where you went, what you did, who you met, and how it went.

Knowledge (keep it light)

Focused concept: a few ready-to-use past-event phrases.

Memorize this mini-exchange:

  1. Ayer fui al mercado.
  2. Hable con una familia.
  3. Compartimos un mensaje sencillo.
  4. Fue un buen dia.

Reference sheet: Past Events

Guided practice (retrieval)

Say each line out loud from memory before looking back up:

Prompt A

Yesterday I went to the market.

Prompt B

I spoke with a family.

Prompt C

We shared a simple message.

Prompt D

It was a good day.

Change mercado to parque and familia to vecino. Tell the story twice.

End-of-lesson assessment

Type each answer from memory. Mastery target for this lesson: 4/4.

Primary source for this lesson

Use SpanishPod101 to repeat the phrases aloud, then recount yesterday in your own words.

Why this source: structured, beginner-friendly, and practical for quick daily sessions.

Spaced follow-up (tomorrow, 3 minutes)

  • Tell a three-line story about yesterday.
  • Change one place and one person.
  • Review any missed line and retell without reading.
Ask follow-up questions: Ask your teacher-agent for a past-visit interview.
