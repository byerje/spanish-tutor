Lesson 0017: Feelings and Needs

Target time: 5-10 minutes. Mission link: notice a person's condition and offer practical help.

Tangible win for today: You can ask how someone feels, say that you are tired or worried, and offer help.

Knowledge (keep it light)

Focused concept: caring questions with como te sientes and necesitas.

Memorize this mini-exchange:

  1. Como te sientes hoy?
  2. Estoy un poco cansado.
  3. Necesitas ayuda?
  4. Si, gracias. Eso es muy amable.

Reference sheet: Feelings and Needs

Guided practice (retrieval)

Say each line out loud from memory before looking back up:

Prompt A

How do you feel today?

Prompt B

I am a little tired.

Prompt C

Do you need help?

Prompt D

Yes, thank you. That is very kind.

Change cansado to preocupado or bien. Practice with a calm voice.

End-of-lesson assessment

Type each answer from memory. Mastery target for this lesson: 4/4.

Primary source for this lesson

Repeat caring questions with SpanishPod101, focusing on natural tone more than speed.

Why this source: structured, beginner-friendly, and practical for quick daily sessions.

Spaced follow-up (tomorrow, 3 minutes)

  • Ask one person how they feel.
  • Offer one specific kind of help.
  • Review any missed line and repeat with a partner.
Ask follow-up questions: Ask your teacher-agent for a compassionate care conversation.
