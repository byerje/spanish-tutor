Tangible win for today: You can ask what someone is doing today, say what you are going to do, and ask a simple follow-up question.

Knowledge (keep it light)

Memorize this mini-exchange:

  1. Que hace usted hoy?
  2. Hoy voy a trabajar.
  3. Y usted?
  4. Hoy voy a visitar a una familia.

Reference sheet: Talking About Today

Guided practice (retrieval)

Say each line out loud from memory before looking back up:

Prompt A

What are you doing today? (formal)

Prompt B

Today I am going to work.

Prompt C

And you?

Prompt D

Today I am going to visit a family.

Replace trabajar and visitar a una familia with one true plan from your day. Say the exchange twice.
