Lesson 0004: Likes and Preferences

Target time: 5-10 minutes. Mission link: build rapport by asking about familiar interests.

Tangible win for today: You can ask what someone likes, say what you like, and respond politely when your preferences are different.

Knowledge (keep it light)

Memorize this mini-exchange:

  1. Que le gusta hacer?
  2. Me gusta escuchar musica.
  3. A mi me gusta leer.
  4. A mi tambien.

Reference sheet: Likes and Preferences

Guided practice (retrieval)

Say each line out loud from memory before looking back up:

Prompt A

What do you like to do?

Prompt B

I like to listen to music.

Prompt C

I like to read.

Prompt D

Me too.

Substitute one activity you genuinely enjoy. Ask the question, answer it, and repeat the exchange twice.

End-of-lesson assessment

Type each answer from memory. Mastery target for this lesson: 4/4.

Primary source for this lesson

Continue with the early lessons of Language Transfer: Complete Spanish. Repeat each short phrase out loud and connect it to something you genuinely enjoy.

Why this source: free, structured, speech-first, and practical for quick daily sessions.

Spaced follow-up (tomorrow, 3 minutes)

  • Ask and answer the four-line exchange from memory twice.
  • Replace the activities with two of your real interests.
  • Review any missed line, then repeat the full exchange once more.
Ask follow-up questions: Ask your teacher-agent for a pronunciation check, an activity substitution drill, or a roleplay about hobbies.
